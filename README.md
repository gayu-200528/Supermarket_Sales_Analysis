# \# **Supermarket Sales Analysis**


###### \## 📌 **Project Overview**


\- This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales data to understand sales patterns, product line performance, and customer preferences.

\- It demonstrates a data analytics workflow, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), data visualization, and business insight generation using Python.

\- The project uses charts and statistical analysis to explore sales distribution, payment methods, and customer ratings.



###### \## 🎯 **Project Objectives**

\- Understand and explore supermarket sales data.

\- Clean and preprocess the dataset.

\- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

\- Visualize sales data using Matplotlib and Seaborn.

\- Analyze sales by product line and payment method.

\- Study correlations between numerical variables.

\- Compare average customer ratings across product lines.

 ## 📂 **Dataset Information**


\- \*\*Dataset Name:\*\* Supermarket Sales Dataset(with missing value)

\- \*\*File Name:\*\* `supermarket\_sales.csv`

\- \*\*Rows:\*\* 1,000

\- \*\*Columns:\*\* 17


###### \*\*Key Features:\*\*

\- \*\*Product Information:\*\* Product line

\- \*\*Sales Information:\*\* Unit price, Quantity, Total, gross income

\- \*\*Payment Information:\*\* Payment

\- \*\*Customer Information:\*\* Gender, Customer type, Rating

\- \*\*Transaction Information:\*\* Invoice ID, Date, Time, Branch, City
